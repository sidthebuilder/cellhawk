1. Methodology: Non-Linear Least Squares

NEXUS-7 navigates by solving the "Emitter Location" problem in reverse. Using Time Difference of Arrival (TDOA) and RSSI from terrestrial towers, the engine calculates the aircraft's 3D position without external satellite aid.

We implement a Trust Region Reflective (TRF) solver. Unlike simple trilateration, TRF is robust to the high non-linearity of radio propagation in urban and mountainous terrain.

2. Resilience: Multipath & GDOP Mitigation

The primary risk in cellular positioning is Multipath Propagation (signals reflecting off buildings). NEXUS-7 mitigates this using two specific layers:

2.1 Soft-L1 Robust Loss Function

By using soft_l1 instead of standard squared residuals, the optimizer effectively ignores "outlier" towers that provide impossible distances due to signal bouncing.

2.2 GDOP Verification

The engine calculates the Geometric Dilution of Precision (GDOP). If towers are arranged in a straight line, the engine automatically fuses high-rate IMU data to bridge the accuracy gap until a better geometry is found.

3. Multi-Sensor Fusion

The positioning engine is Tightly Coupled. We fuse cellular residuals directly with the Extended Kalman Filter (EKF) at 1000Hz, ensuring smooth flight dynamics even during tower handoffs.
